How ordering works

Order

Build your cart here, then tap Order on WhatsApp. Your order arrives in our chat, ready to send. We confirm your size and delivery in the chat before you pay.

Size guide

Women's pieces come in sizes 4 to 22, depending on the style. Men's sets come in S to XXL. Some styles cost a little more from size 20. Not sure of your size? Ask us on WhatsApp before you order.

Delivery

We ship from Lagos to anywhere in the world. Delivery time and cost depend on where you are, and we confirm both in the chat.
